THE WATCHERS OF THE REALM

STAFF HIERARCHY

The staff team exists to protect the server, enforce its standards and maintain order throughout the realm.

LEVEL I
⚕️ STAFF

Staff

Higher moderators responsible for inspecting the actions of departments and watching the server for members who violate its rules.

LEVEL II
📜 SENIOR STAFF

Senior Staff

Leaders of the Staff team. They guide and correct staff members and possess the authority to discipline staff when necessary.

LEVEL III
🔮 ADMINISTRATOR

Administrator

Managers of the Staff team and the second-highest authority within the staff hierarchy. They oversee the server, prevent rule violations from going unchecked, issue announcements and host server events.

LEVEL IV
👑 HEAD ADMINISTRATOR

Head Administrator 🔮

The pinnacle of the Staff hierarchy. This position can only be held by two people. Head Administrators manage the Administrators, issue announcements, host both in-game and server events and reward members who perform exceptionally in tournaments.
