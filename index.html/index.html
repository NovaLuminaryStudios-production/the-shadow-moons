THE STAFF HIERARCHY

STAFF TEAM

The staff structure exists to maintain order, enforce the rules and ensure the guild remains properly managed.

⚕️

Staff

SERVER MODERATION

Higher moderators responsible for inspecting the actions of departments and monitoring the server for rule violations.

📜

Senior Staff

STAFF LEADERSHIP

Leads and guides the Staff team. Senior Staff can correct and discipline Staff members when necessary.

🔮

Administrator

SERVER MANAGEMENT

Managers of the Staff team with superior authority over Senior Staff. Administrators monitor the server, issue announcements and host server events.

👑

Head Administrator

PINNACLE OF STAFF

The highest role within the Staff team. This role is limited to two people and oversees Administrators, announcements, events and rewards.
