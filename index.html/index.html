ORDER & DISCIPLINE

STAFF TEAM

The official staff hierarchy responsible for monitoring the server, maintaining order, managing staff, hosting events, and rewarding members who perform well.

👑 HIGHEST STAFF AUTHORITY

Head Administrator 🔮

The pinnacle of hierarchy within the Staff Team. This role can only be handled by two people. Head Administrators manage the Administrators, give announcements, host both in-game and server events, and give rewards to members who participate well in tournaments.

Maximum: Two Holders
🔮 SENIOR MANAGEMENT

Administrator

Administrators act as managers to the Staff Team. They oversee the server, prevent rule-breakers from going unchecked, give announcements, and host server events.

Second Highest Staff Authority
📜 STAFF LEADERSHIP

Senior Staff

Senior Staff are above the Staff rank and are responsible for leading, guiding, and correcting Staff members. They hold full authority to discipline Staff members when necessary.

Staff Leadership
⚕️ MODERATION

Staff

Higher moderators whose duty is to inspect the actions of departments and monitor the server to identify members who are breaking the rules.
