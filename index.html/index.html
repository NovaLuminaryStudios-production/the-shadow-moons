THE ADMINISTRATIVE HIERARCHY

STAFF TEAM

The staff structure exists to maintain order, discipline and activity throughout the server.

⚕️

Staff

SERVER MODERATION

Higher moderators responsible for inspecting departmental activity and watching the server for members breaking the rules.

📜

Senior Staff

STAFF LEADERSHIP

Leads and guides the Staff team. Senior Staff can correct and discipline Staff members when necessary.

🔮

Administrator

SERVER MANAGEMENT

Managers of the Staff team. Administrators watch over the server, enforce rules, give announcements and host server events.

👑

Head Administrator

PINNACLE OF STAFF

The highest position within the Staff hierarchy. Limited to two people. They manage Administrators, give announcements, host events and distribute rewards to members who perform well in tournaments.
